\begin{letter}{Editor\\
Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics}

\opening{Dear Editors,}

I am writing to resubmit our manuscript entitled \textbf{“A new framework for non-stationary spatio-temporal data fusion of multi-fidelity models”} for consideration in the \textit{Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics}.

This manuscript is a thoroughly revised version of our previous submission (Submission ID: \textbf{256296463} and man Manuscript ID JCGS-25-682), which was handled by the Editor \textbf{Professor Laura Maria Sangalli} (Politecnico di Milano).

In the decision letter, we were informed that:

\begin{quote}
“Please note that the Associate Editor has left open the possibility for you to resubmit a thoroughly revised version of your manuscript. Any resubmission will be treated as a new submission, with no guarantee of acceptance. If you choose to proceed, please reference the previous submission ID in your cover letter and provide a detailed, point-by-point response to the reviewers' and editor's comments.”
\end{quote}

We have carefully followed this guidance in preparing the present revision. In particular, we have undertaken a comprehensive revision of the manuscript to address all concerns raised by the reviewers and the Associate Editor.

The main changes include:

\begin{itemize}
\item A reformulation of the methodological framework using a Generalized Least Squares approach;
\item The introduction of new simulation experiments specifically designed to assess approximation accuracy and error propagation;
\item A redesigned and expanded empirical evaluation, including improved validation strategies and sensitivity analyses;
\item Additional theoretical clarification, including explicit treatment of covariance structure and positive semi-definiteness;
\item A detailed description of the optimization procedure and implementation choices to enhance reproducibility;
\item A complete restructuring of the manuscript to improve clarity, coherence, and organization.
\end{itemize}

We have also provided a detailed, point-by-point response to all comments in the accompanying document.

We believe that these revisions fully address the concerns raised in the previous review process and significantly strengthen the manuscript. We respectfully submit this revised version for consideration as a new submission, as indicated in the decision letter.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

\closing{Sincerely,}

\vspace{1cm}

Pietro Colombo\\
(on behalf of all co-authors)

\end{letter}
